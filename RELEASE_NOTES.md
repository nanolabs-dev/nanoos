nanoOS v1.0.1 rebuilds the app and sharing files under the nanoLabs identity.

- New QR PNG and SVG use https://nanolabs-dev.github.io/nanoos/ and contain the updated application.
- Compressed app: 2,790 bytes; Version 40-L QR; 4,226-character URL.
- New desktop and mobile screenshots, launchers, sharing ZIP and verification reports.
- Verified QR decoding, live Chrome behavior, notes persistence and offline operation after boot.
- Existing license restrictions remain in force. The official QR may be shared unchanged with attribution.

Notes from older QR payloads are not automatically migrated. Physical phone-camera scanning and Safari have not been verified. This release updates current files; it does not by itself remove historical copies or cached commits.
